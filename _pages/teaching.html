Teaching practice

How I approach teaching

01

Explain the reason

Students learn technical details more effectively when they understand the problem those details solve.

02

Build from small examples

Focused examples make a new idea visible; complete programs show how the pieces work together.

03

Teach debugging

I ask students to inspect program state, test assumptions, and explain why a correction works.

04

Make questions welcome

A respectful classroom helps students ask for help before confusion becomes discouragement.

05

Connect theory and practice

My research and software-engineering experience help me show how course concepts appear in real systems.

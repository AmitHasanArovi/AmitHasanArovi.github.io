For these courses, I design lectures and labs, prepare programming assignments and assessments, coordinate with teaching assistants, hold office hours, and support students with varied levels of prior computing experience. ## Penn State As a teaching assistant, I led recitations and held office hours for: - **CMPSC 311: Introduction to Systems Programming** — Spring 2023 and Spring 2025 - **CMPSC 473: Operating Systems Design and Construction** — Fall 2023 I helped students work through C and C++ programming, Unix systems programming, memory management, concurrency, and operating-systems projects. ## How I approach teaching - **Explain the reason, not only the rule.** Students learn technical details more effectively when they understand the problem those details solve. - **Move from small examples to complete programs.** Short examples make a new idea visible; larger assignments show how the pieces work together. - **Treat debugging as part of learning.** I ask students to read compiler messages, inspect program state, test assumptions, and explain why a correction works. - **Make questions welcome.** Students arrive with different levels of preparation. A respectful classroom helps them ask for help before confusion becomes discouragement. - **Connect theory with practice.** My research and software-engineering experience help me show how course concepts appear in real systems. I am prepared to teach introductory programming, data structures and algorithms, systems programming, operating systems, computer architecture and assembly language, concurrency and parallel computing, and advanced courses in non-blocking algorithms and memory management.
